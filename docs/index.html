All your Gmail accounts in one inbox. Flyver is a free Chrome extension that brings several Gmail accounts together in one popup.

Add to Chrome

What you can do

  • Read the newest mail from all your accounts in one list
  • Reply, forward, send, mark as read and move mail to trash
  • Search across all accounts at once
  • See the total number of unread messages on the toolbar icon

Private by design

Flyver runs entirely in your browser. It talks only to Google, has no servers of its own, and doesn't collect, sell or share your data. Read the privacy policy.

Open source

The full source code is on GitHub. Contributions are welcome.
