Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Flyver Chrome extension (“Flyver”) handles your data. In short: your mail stays between your browser and Google. Flyver has no servers of its own and doesn't collect, store, sell or share your data with anyone.

What Flyver accesses

When you sign in with a Google account, you allow Flyver to use these Google permissions:

  • Gmail (read and modify): show, search, send, reply to, forward, mark as read and trash your mail when you ask it to.
  • Your email address: tell your accounts apart in the extension.

Where your data goes

  • Flyver talks directly from your browser to Google (accounts.google.com, gmail.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com). No other server is involved.
  • Your mail is shown in the extension and is not stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Flyver stores your account email addresses, short-lived access tokens (valid for about one hour) and your chosen theme color in your browser's local extension storage on your own device. Flyver stores no passwords.
  • Flyver doesn't use analytics, tracking, ads or cookies of its own.
  • Images inside emails may be loaded from the sender's servers when you open a mail, just like in Gmail.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Flyver's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide Flyver's features to you. It is never transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or read by humans.

How to delete your data

  • Remove an account in Flyver's settings, or uninstall the extension. This deletes everything Flyver has stored.
  • Revoke Flyver's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
