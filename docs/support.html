Common questions

How do I add another Gmail account?
Open Flyver, click the settings icon and choose to add an account. Sign in with the account you want to add.

Flyver asks me to sign in again.
Flyver renews access automatically. If you sign out of Google or remove Flyver's access, you have to sign in again from the settings.

How do I remove an account?
Remove it in Flyver's settings. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Is my data safe?
Flyver talks only to Google and has no servers of its own. See the privacy policy.
